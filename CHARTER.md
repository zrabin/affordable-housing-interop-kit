# Charter

## Why this exists

Affordable housing applications in New York pass between applicants, marketing agents, owners, compliance teams, and public agencies through handoffs that are slow, manual, and opaque. Everyone in this ecosystem, whatever software they use or sell, shares an interest in applications that move faster, data that stays accurate, and a process every participant can trust.

The Open Housing Data Standard is a public, open effort to give that shared interest a shared language. This charter states the principles the effort is built on and defines exactly what it means to support it.

## The principles

1. **Interoperability.** Housing application data should be able to move between the systems that applicants, operators, and agencies choose, in a documented, machine-readable form.
2. **Tenant consent.** Applicant data moves only under a bounded, purpose-specific grant from the applicant. Consent is a first-class part of the data model, not a checkbox.
3. **Auditability.** Every meaningful action on an application, by a person or by software, is recorded in an append-only trail and can be reconstructed.
4. **Vendor neutrality.** No single company controls the interface. Any compliant system can implement it, and no implementer owes anything to any other.
5. **Openness.** The schemas, the specifications, and the discussion that shapes them happen in public.

## What supporting means

- Your organization publicly endorses the five principles above and is listed in [SUPPORTERS.md](SUPPORTERS.md).
- You are invited into the conversation: feedback on the Standard, working sessions, and the planned convening of supporters.

## What supporting does not mean

- **No fees.** Supporting costs nothing.
- **No exclusivity.** Supporting does not limit any vendor relationship, procurement choice, or partnership.
- **No adoption obligation.** Supporting does not commit you to implement the Standard or change any system you run.
- **No governance obligation.** Helping govern the Standard (co-stewardship) is a separate, opt-in path described in [docs/governance.md](docs/governance.md).
- **No vendor endorsement.** Supporting the Standard is not an endorsement of Harmony or of any other implementer.
